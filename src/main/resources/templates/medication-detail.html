Possible Side Effects

Side effects description

Side effects vary by individual. Common side effects may include:

  • Nausea or stomach upset
  • Dizziness or drowsiness
  • Allergic reactions in some individuals
  • Other side effects as described in medication leaflet
Seek immediate medical help if you experience:
  • Difficulty breathing or swallowing
  • Severe skin rash or itching
  • Swelling of face, lips, or tongue
  • Severe dizziness or fainting

Precautions & Warnings

Precautions description

  • Inform your doctor about all medications you're taking
  • Disclose any allergies or medical conditions
  • Avoid alcohol unless approved by your doctor
  • Not recommended during pregnancy without medical advice
  • Keep out of reach of children
  • Do not share medication with others
