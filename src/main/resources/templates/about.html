Our Mission

CareBeforeCare is dedicated to providing immediate, accessible, and reliable pre-hospital care information to everyone, everywhere.

In emergency situations, every second counts. Our platform is designed to deliver crucial medical guidance when professional help may be minutes away. We believe that empowering individuals with timely knowledge can make life-saving differences.

Emergency Preparedness

Equipping everyday individuals with standard medical instructions to handle unexpected health events with calm and confidence.

Important Medical Disclaimer

CareBeforeCare is an educational platform and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

  • This platform is designed entirely for educational and informational purposes only.
  • Always consult qualified healthcare professionals or physicians for physical symptoms, medical diagnosis, and treatment.
  • In case of a real life-threatening emergency, please call your local emergency number (108) immediately.
  • Do not delay seeking professional clinical advice based on information read from this platform.
