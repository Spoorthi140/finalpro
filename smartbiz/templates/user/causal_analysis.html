How to interpret these results?

Traditional analytics shows correlations (e.g., "when price goes up, sales go down"). Causal AI shows the impact of a specific action while controlling for other factors.

  • Positive Effect: Increasing the treatment variable causes the outcome to increase.
  • Negative Effect: Increasing the treatment variable causes the outcome to decrease.
  • Zero Effect: The treatment variable has no causal impact on the outcome.
